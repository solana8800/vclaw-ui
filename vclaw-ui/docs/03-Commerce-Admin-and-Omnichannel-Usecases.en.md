At VClaw, we believe technology should be a powerful ally, not a barrier. You don't need to know programming; you just need to know your business. All complex tasks are handled in the background, and you simply act as the **Approver**.

## 2. Real-World Business Scenarios (Use Cases)

### 2.1 Omnichannel Customer Intake
Your customers are everywhere: Zalo, Facebook, Shopee... VClaw helps you gather them all into a "Unified Inbox":
- **Benefit**: Never miss a message; no need to switch back and forth between apps.
- **How to use**: Follow the **Conversations** section to reply to customers from all sources in one single place.

### 2.2 Assisted Selling
When a customer asks about products or pricing:
- AI will suggest response templates that match your shop's style.
- **Fast VietQR Creation**: AI automatically recognizes the amount from the conversation and creates a QR code with the order details. You just click once to send it to the customer.

### 2.3 Lightning-Fast Bill Verification
This is VClaw's most "powerful" feature:
- When a customer sends a payment screenshot, the AI automatically reads (OCR) it to check: **Is the amount correct? Is the content right?**
- **Result**: The system displays a green "Matched" notification. You just click **[Approve Order]**. No more opening your banking app to squint at every transaction.

### 2.4 Shipping Assist & Quotes
- **Smart Address Reading**: When a customer chats their address, the AI automatically breaks it down into Province, District, Ward...
- **Real-time Shipping Quotes**: VClaw connects with carriers (GHTK, GHN...) to get real-time shipping prices. You can immediately quote the shipping fee to the customer without manual lookups.

### 2.5 Booking & Reminders
For service-based shops (Spas, Salons, Appointments):
- AI helps you record appointments directly from the chat.
- **Automated Reminders**: The system will automatically message the customer to remind them before the appointment (e.g., 2 hours before) to ensure they arrive on time.

## 3. Managing Work Through Flows

VClaw helps you track an order's lifecycle visually:
1. **Inquiry**: Consulting phase.
2. **Wait Pay**: VietQR code sent.
3. **Paid**: AI verified the bill successfully.
4. **Processing**: Order pushed to the carrier.
5. **Done**: Customer received the goods.

You can manage these statuses easily through the **Kanban** interface (dragging and dropping order cards).

---

## 4. Security & Privacy

We understand that customer data is your greatest asset:
- **Local Storage**: All data stays on your computer; no one can access it but you.
- **Total Control**: You can backup or wipe data at any time using the included **Uninstall** utility.
